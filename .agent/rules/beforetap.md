# Antigravity project rule

Follow [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) as the canonical project contract. Read [STATUS.md](../../STATUS.md) first, then only documents relevant to the task.

Before modifying files, propose a bounded plan. After modifying, show the diff summary, testing evidence and what remains unverified. Do not create a full app in one prompt. Respect platform-specific Figma designs and the four-verdict safety model.
